A calculator whose history works like a spreadsheet. Type maths as you would write it: every line gets a number, so you can reuse an answer as #12, add up a selection or copy lines as a table. Notes, share tools, units, graphs and 3D solids are all in one app.
Try "100 km/h to mph" or "solve(x^2 − 5x + 6 = 0)": answers can stay exact, with fractions, surds and π.

■ Like a spreadsheet
• Tap a line's number for #12; edit a line in the line itself or in the field below, like a cell and the formula bar, and the lines that use it are worked out again
• Sum and average of the lines you select; copy them as a table
• Paste from a spreadsheet: add it up, or make lines of the history, of the notes, statistics data or a matrix
• Calculation notes with a total, names (rent = 2400 AUD) and #3; table mode with formula columns (QCalc Pro)

■ 3D drawn by the GPU (QCalc Pro)
• Real light and shadows, metals and liquids; "See-through", also for one part
• Turn it any way with one finger. Without QCalc Pro, open 3D once for free

■ For students and teachers
• Graphs of functions and of parametric, polar and implicit curves, with sliders; tangents, roots, turning points and areas on the graph
• "⟳ 3D" turns the region between two curves into a solid of revolution with its volume; discs and shells, cross-sections
• Equations and simultaneous equations, differentiation, integration, limits, sums, series, matrices up to 30 × 30, complex numbers, binary and hex
• Statistics: distributions, histogram, box plot, regression (curves: QCalc Pro). Up to 200,000 digits (over 1,000: QCalc Pro)

■ For work on site and in design
• Areas and volumes of 22 shapes, liquid in tanks (level ⇄ volume), weight from density
• Units attached (4.2 m × 3.6 m to m², 2.4 m × 1.8 m × 0.15 m to L), error propagation
• QCalc Pro: shapes and tanks in 3D, tank heads, solids from a measured profile, STL files

■ Shares and investing
• Profit and loss with fees and tax, average cost; yield, P/E, P/B, ROE, compound growth
• QCalc Pro: position size from a stop-loss, the margin ratio and margin-call price
• Only the numbers you enter, no prices fetched. Estimates, not investment advice

■ For shopping and the household budget
• Price with a discount and 10 % GST, unit prices, splitting a bill, home loan repayments with a table, dates (days from today, age), hours and pay
• Currencies at rates from the European Central Bank and others

■ Easy to use
• Examples in every tool; exact and approximate answers on two lines
• Next steps under the answer; search, pin and note lines; memory keys
• A home screen widget, a quick settings tile; share a graph as a picture
• Seven languages, light and dark. The manual is in the app, offline too

■ QCalc Pro (one in-app purchase)
3D, more than 1,000 digits, margin and position sizing, the table mode and more notes, regression curves, tank heads, profiles, STL, CSV, colour themes. Everything for learning and everyday use works without it. The price is shown on the Google Play purchase screen.

■ Privacy and openness
• There are no ads and no subscriptions.
• All calculations run on your phone, and it works offline too; the internet is used only for exchange rates.
• QCalc collects no personal data and sends nothing to the developer. Your history and settings stay on your phone.
• Open source (GNU GPL v2 or later): https://github.com/HiroshiTsu/qcalc
• QCalc uses libqalculate, the open-source calculation engine of Qalculate!. QCalc is made independently by one person and is not affiliated with the Qalculate! developers.

■ Please note
Results are for reference. Do not use them for trade or certification, design and construction, or tax returns; check important results by an official method.
